# Interpretation glossary: Maharashtra government resolutions

How Niyam reads recurring legal phrases. The agent reads this file before
extracting or resolving, and adds an entry whenever it settles an ambiguity.
Each entry cites where the interpretation came from. Entries marked
"needs human confirmation" have not been confirmed by a person yet.

## "पेक्षा जास्त नसावे" (should not be more than)
Inclusive upper limit: a value equal to the limit is allowed.
Source: GR 202406281814018230, clause 4(5), income "not more than Rs 2.50 lakh".

## "पेक्षा जास्त" (more than)
Strict comparison: a value equal to the limit is not "more than" the limit.
Source: GR 202406281814018230, clauses 5(4) and 5(7).

## "किंवा त्यापेक्षा जास्त" (or more than that)
Inclusive: a value equal to the limit counts.

## "वयाची N वर्षे पूर्ण होईपर्यंत" (until N years of age are completed)
Niyam stores ages as completed years and treats the upper age N as eligible
(age N is inside the range). This matches the scheme summary in the same GR,
"21 ते 60 या वर्ष वयोगटातील" (in the 21 to 60 age group).
Status: needs human confirmation. A strict reading could end eligibility on the
Nth birthday.

## "कुटुंब" (family)
Not yet defined in the rulebook. Niyam treats family facts (income, tax payer,
government job, land, vehicle) as reported by the applicant for her household.

## Dates
A GR applies from its own date unless it states another start date.
A change to an earlier GR keeps the earlier value in history (a new dated value
is added; old values are never edited).

## "शासन निर्णयामध्ये खालीलप्रमाणे सुधारणा" (the GR is amended as follows)
An amending GR changes only the clauses it lists. Every other provision of the original GR stays in force, including how the benefit amount is calculated (for example, paying only the difference when another scheme already pays less than Rs 1,500). When the amending GR merely repeats an amount, as in 'दरमहा रु.१,५००/-' in its deadline clause, that does not cancel a top-up rule in the original GR.
Source: GR 202407031335114330, opening of 'शासन निर्णय' and section ड; benefit form from GR 202406281814018230 (needs human confirmation of the exact clause).
