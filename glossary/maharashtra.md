# Interpretation glossary: Maharashtra government resolutions

How Niyam reads recurring legal phrases. Generic entries only.

## "पेक्षा जास्त नसावे" (should not be more than)
Inclusive upper limit: a value equal to the limit is allowed.

## "पेक्षा जास्त" (more than)
Strict comparison: a value equal to the limit is not "more than" the limit.

## "किंवा त्यापेक्षा जास्त" (or more than that)
Inclusive: a value equal to the limit counts.

## "वयाची N वर्षे पूर्ण होईपर्यंत" (until N years of age are completed)
Store ages as completed years and treat age N as inside the range, unless the order says otherwise. Flag for human confirmation.

## Dates
A GR applies from its own date unless it states another start date.
